## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
We propose a digital platform that connects farmers directly with customers, shops and wholesalers without unnecessary middlemen.
- Farmers can upload their products and prices.
- Buyers can directly purchase products from farmers.
- AI helps farmers predict product demand and market prices.
- The platform helps arrange transportation and delivery.
- Customers can buy fresh products at lower prices.
The main aim is to increase farmers' income and reduce the cost for consumers.

## Technical Approach
1. Technologies to Be Used
- HTML, CSS and JavaScript – Website development.
- Python – Backend development.
- MySQL – Storing user and product details.
- Machine Learning – Predicting product demand and prices.
- Google Maps – Finding delivery routes.
2. Working Process
1. Farmers register on the website.
2. Farmers upload their products and prices.
3. Buyers select the products they need.
4. AI predicts demand and suggests prices.
5. Transportation is arranged.
6. Products are delivered to buyers.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Feasibility of the Idea
- The website can be developed using simple technologies.
- Farmers can use it through mobile phones.
- It can be expanded to different villages and cities.
2. Challenges
- Some farmers may not know how to use the website.
- Internet facilities may not be available everywhere.
- Transportation may be expensive.
3. Solutions
- Provide a simple and easy-to-use website.
- Support local languages.
- Connect with nearby transport providers.
- Provide training to farmers.

## Impact and Benefits
1. Impact on People
- Farmers can earn more money.
- Consumers can buy products at lower prices.
- Shops can purchase products directly from farmers.
2. Benefits
- Reduces middlemen.
- Saves time and money.
- Provides fresh agricultural products.
- Reduces food wastage.
- Supports farmers and rural development.
